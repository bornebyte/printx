# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want PrintX to be a welcoming, respectful, and technically constructive project. Contributors are expected to:

- Be considerate and patient with people of different backgrounds and experience.
- Give and receive feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Prefer clear, inclusive language and assume good intent while resolving disagreement.
- Respect privacy, security, and the data handled by the platform.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's private GitHub contact or security channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe.
